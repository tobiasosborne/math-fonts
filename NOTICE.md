AGPL does not replace their upstream licenses. Refer to each file's header.

Storm Baskerville 10 Pro is a supplied commercial reference, not part of this
repository's redistributable font payload. Source font binaries, locally
rendered commercial reference specimens, embedded-font proof PDFs and private
backup material are excluded from version control. Reproduction requires
providing the reference fonts separately. No foundry endorsement is implied.

Earlier foundry-inspired image experiments are project-generated studies,
not original works supplied or approved by the named foundries. The current
image-derived outlines are an experiment, not a released complete math font.

# Upright O — third pilot enlargement

One built-in imagegen call, no retry or correction. The unified foundation
root and its exact O crop define the drawing; substantial actual Storm text
samples provide family context. Root and original RGB source remain unchanged.

Inspection of the registered overlay confirms the oval construction, thick
sides and fine top/bottom strokes. The enlarged oval is slightly narrower,
with counter/outer-curve drift: master overlap 92.51%, mean boundary distance
0.68 source pixels, p95 1.41 pixels, aspect -2.66%, ink area -1.34%.
Registration permits translation and uniform scale only, with no independent
width fitting. These diagnostics measure source agreement, not design quality.

Full-component tracing preserves one ink component and one counter. Its nine
cubic segments have 99.23% native-input silhouette overlap. Actual vectors,
control handles, curves, metrics and rerender are saved under `trace/`.
Explicit luminance conversion is recorded; clean RGB input is preserved and
no annotation guides are flattened into its silhouette.

Retain as a **pilot candidate**. Review oval width, stress, optical overshoot,
curvature and weight together with H/A in a shared-size font proof before
release acceptance. A low node count is not a quality guarantee; neither
tangent continuity nor high raster overlap establishes a finished drawing.
No correction budget is spent now, and this enlargement does not replace or
redefine the unified root. Connected-only registration is valid for O; it
must not be reused unmodified for future disconnected symbols.

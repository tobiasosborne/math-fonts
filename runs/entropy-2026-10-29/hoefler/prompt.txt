Emphasize crisp modulated stress, restrained decisive terminals, poised joins, economical strong silhouettes, an incisive but fluid true Greek italic, controlled fit and extraordinarily lucid white. Concrete form opportunities: asymmetrical yet poised beta bowls and a clean waist; alpha with a deliberate crossing and living counter; theta with an open oval and carefully weighted bar; xi with three distinct rhythmic turns rather than stacked black knots; lambda with a decisive rise and elegant entry; nu with a clearly forward Greek gesture and pointed-but-not-pinched turn; upsilon with a distinct rounded bowl/terminal; omega with balanced spacious lobes and a controlled central join; phi with open counters around an assured stem. Develop these ideas across the WHOLE alphabet and four styles; don't just redesign nu. Do not force Latin serif anatomy onto Greek. Bold must be its own carefully articulated companion, not simply swollen regular strokes. Small script forms need visibly more resilient counters, fit and articulation without becoming crude.

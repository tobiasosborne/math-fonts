# Hoefler & Co.-inspired exploration

[Original image](redesigned.png), [exact prompt](prompt.txt), and
[provenance](provenance.json). One built-in imagegen call used the same exact
retained-0.152 reference as the other directions. This is independent AI work,
not a Hoefler & Co. commission or a copy of Mercury Text.

The parent supplied the primary reference notes for
[Mercury Text](https://www.typography.com/fonts/mercury-text) and
[Jonathan Hoefler's typefaces](https://jonathanhoefler.com/typefaces).
Related optical roles and grades informed the brief. The precise synthesis
shown here is a creative proposal, not a rule attributed to the foundry.

The vector designer could construct the enlarged ν's forward, open rising
gesture while keeping its pointed base and the rounder υ. The unequal β bowls,
poised waist, broad θ counter and slightly rising crossbar, three articulated ξ
turns, and balanced ω lobes offer a coordinated set of editorial forms. In bold,
interpret the open counters and firm terminals glyph by glyph rather than
applying a uniform outline offset. Preserve Greek anatomy and the protected
Latin/radical system.

Reject the image's Latin-v control: it becomes a serif capital-like V rather
than the actual Baskerville italic v. Do not use its weak κ/ϰ or ε/ϵ separation
as a character map. The alphabet retains parts of the input, so the new layout
and attractive details are not evidence of a fully redesigned family. Labels
claiming text/script sizes are conceptual, not actual optical masters or
physical-size proofs.

Verification belongs in the native font: compare the complete Greek set and
variants on the fixed grids, natural 8/10/12 pt text and real scripts, against
the unchanged Latin v and actual Baskerville prose. The ν–υ cue must survive
8 pt nested indices without a dark foot. β/θ/ξ/ω should sustain open white and
even texture in bold and at distance, while other glyphs, advances, anchors,
Greek distinctions and radical constructions retain their intended behaviour.

The original output is 1054 × 1492, below the requested resolution target; it
was saved without upscaling. The simplified diffusion and Gaussian formulas
look coherent, but exact mathematics, glyph coverage and font quality are not
certified by this raster. No actual font or council artifact changed.

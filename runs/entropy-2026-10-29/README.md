# Štorm-inspired exploration

One independent Codex image-design experiment, requested as constructive entropy.
This is an AI concept, not work by František Štorm and not a compiled font.

- [Exact current-design reference](current-design-600dpi.png): 4961 × 7016 PNG,
  one A4 page at 600 dpi, rendered from retained 0.152 and Baskerville 10 Pro.
  Full Greek/variants in four serif styles, realistic heat-kernel, Gaussian and
  membrane mathematics, prose, accents and nested scripts. PDF/TEX/font copies
  remain in `private/`; embedding and missing/overfull checks passed.
- [Generated visual redesign](redesigned-storm-inspired.png): the original
  1055 × 1491 result of one built-in imagegen call, without upscaling or edits.
- [Exact prompt](prompt.txt) and [provenance](provenance.json).

Useful starting points are the broader calligraphic ν gesture, the clear
pointed/rounded ν–υ contrast in the enlarged row, expansive counter ideas and
a coordinated family treatment. These are prompts for the font designer to
interpret, not outlines to trace or replacements to install automatically.

The model retained much of the reference layout and produced a lower-resolution
image than the reference. Its heat-kernel integral loses a lower limit, tiny
script identities change, and variant rows are redundant or insufficiently
distinct. No coverage, semantic accuracy, optical performance or typographic
perfection is established by the generated image. The normal native-font
proofing and bounded council selection rules still apply.

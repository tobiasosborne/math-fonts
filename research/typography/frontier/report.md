# Frontier research: math-font evaluation and council plan

2026-10-29. Scope: proposed future work; no font or council has been built. Source IDs resolve in sources.json. Local files are upstream tool documentation and licenses, not evidence that a particular font is good.

## Transfer from Sublimite

The supplied state-of-design, rubric, council and research.json are starting points for evidence tagging, independent review and practical proofing. They concern an editor and its UI: code-cell widths, texture healing, OLED antialiasing, interface aesthetics and editor-size defaults do not establish the right outlines, spacing or evaluation for a Baskerville-derived mathematical face. Monaspace and Iosevka demonstrate configurable code typography, not proportional mathematical typesetting; FreeType describes one rendering path, not a universal visual ground truth. Preserve the discipline of separating measured claims from estimates, but design a new task-specific rubric and corpus; do not import their scores or thresholds.

## Source findings and implications

- [F01, Axis definition and Additional information] `opsz` specifies intended point size; automatic selection normally uses nominal size at 100% zoom. It does not guarantee an attractive or legible design. [F02, Families with Optical Size Variants] recommends STAT ranges. **Proposal:** examine actual text, script and scriptscript sizes, axis extremes and intermediates, keeping nominal size and zoom recorded; do not require an axis before deciding whether it is needed.
- [F03, MathConstants, MathGlyphInfo, MathVariants] MATH encodes formula positioning, glyph corrections and extensible variants; the specification explicitly is not a complete layout algorithm. **Proposal:** proof real equations in actual math layout engines, including nested fractions, radical joins, delimiter growth, scripts, accents, italic correction and dense matrices. Inspect both glyphs and assembled output. Spec suggested script scales (80%, 60%) are examples, not project acceptance thresholds.
- [F04, sections 4–6 and Fig. 13] a controlled prose reading study reports substantial individual differences and uneven sampling of font pairs; its participants, text and conditions do not validate math comprehension or prove a universal best font. **Proposal:** use randomized within-reader comparisons and measure errors and comprehension alongside speed, without assuming preference predicts performance. Confirm the original protocol and statistics before designing a trial.
- [F05, README / License; F06, README] Font Bakery checks technical quality; fontTools inspects and manipulates font data. [F07, Features and Caveats] Diffenator 2 can compare axis locations and word images, but acknowledges wordlists miss combinations and features. **Proposal:** pin tool versions and profiles, document exemptions, inspect visual differences instead of optimizing a diff score, and supplement words with math-specific rendered proofs. A pass is necessary where applicable, never proof of typographic quality.
- [F08, font selection and optical size] application selection behavior varies by family model and platform. **Proposal:** test the intended actual engines, record font, engine, ppem, display scaling and settings; compare print/PDF with screen, not just a FreeType bitmap. Variable MATH support and interpolation behavior require empirical verification per engine.

## Proposed rubric (project choices, not source-derived numerical standards)

Use anchored observations and examples, not a single unqualified aesthetic score. Gate on reproducible build, valid tables, required repertoire as defined by project scope, no missing glyphs or clipping in the agreed corpus, and no serious semantic ambiguity. Record any exceptions and reviewer. Grade remaining dimensions separately: (1) letterform fidelity and coherent Baskerville character without copying historical defects; (2) discriminability of confusable symbols in context; (3) consistent perceived weight and spacing across text and math, regular and italic; (4) math positioning and construction across nesting and sizes; (5) small-size resilience and optical consistency; (6) platform robustness. Assess paragraphs, inline formulas, display equations and scripts at intended physical sizes as well as magnified outlines. No numeric pass level is justified by these sources; set one only after baseline measurement and human calibration.

## Implementation plan for a future authorized hillclimb

1. Define target engines, sizes, languages, coverage, allowed source licenses and baseline font; agree on a reproducible corpus of scientific prose and representative mathematical expressions, with expected semantic interpretations. Capture source provenance and separate development, calibration and concealed holdout sets. Include diverse disciplines and difficult symbol pairs.
2. Establish pinned build and proof pipeline, font metadata/table inspection, chosen QA profiles, and deterministic equation rendering in at least the actual target engine(s). Store environment and output hashes; compare baseline and candidate at matched conditions. Validate tests by deliberately introducing representative defects.
3. Recruit relevant typographers and mathematical readers; create anchored examples and independently rate blinded, randomized A/B pairs. Capture disagreement and reasons before discussion. Repeat a subset with swapped labels and presentations; evaluate consistency and compare model judgments against independent human adjudication on held-out cases. Models may triage defects, not establish human readability.
4. Track separate technical, typographic and reading outcomes. Preregister a small change and the criterion it should improve; reject candidates with serious regressions even if a composite improves. Require human adjudication of disagreements and inspect failed cases. Preserve a no-change baseline; record all candidate versions and the full review trail.
5. Freeze holdout material and periodically rotate newly authored private examples; avoid exposing answers or optimizing against the council's exact prompts. Audit model order, identity, prompt sensitivity and correlated judgments; measure agreement and false negatives on seeded defects, not only average scores. Escalate uncertainty and never count multiple related models as independent human evidence.
6. Pilot a reader study before any power or sample-size commitment. Counterbalance order and expressions, use paired analysis with participant variation and uncertainty intervals, define accessibility and comprehension measures, and predeclare stopping and analysis rules. Speed alone is not success. Seek math-specific empirical literature and expert input before treating this design as validated.

## Limits

These sources do not establish a Baskerville math optimum, thresholds, general model-human agreement, or a validated mathematics-reading test. F04 is prose research, not math; no math-specific readability experiment was verified in this round. Copyrighted Microsoft pages and author-hosted paper are linked and summarized, not redistributed; no book or full copyrighted article was copied. The two downloaded upstream READMEs and accompanying licenses provide local resources, not a font specimen. Source URLs that track a branch can change; hashes preserve the captured bytes. Future work should obtain explicitly licensed math specimens and verify target-engine variable-MATH behavior.

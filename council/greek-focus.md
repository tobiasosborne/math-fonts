# Greek lowercase campaign — proof and grading focus

The absolute editorial scale remains rubric 0.3. This campaign asks whether the
**lowercase Greek alphabet** belongs in a hypothetical perfect Baskerville math
font worthy of a leading typography periodical's featured new-design article.
Judge the declared Greek scope, not a new redesign of unchanged radicals or
Latin. Do not let impressive proof graphics substitute for accomplished type.

Scope includes the 24 letters, final sigma, conventional mathematical variants,
and their upright/italic/bold/bold-italic and optical forms where declared.
An inventory must identify which forms were changed and which were retained.
Preserve Greek anatomy and semantic distinctions; Latin-like serifs, identical
slopes and identical counters are not universal standards of quality.

## Read the proofs like a typographer

- **Working size:** start with unadorned 8/10/12 pt formulas and prose. Look at
  recognition, rhythm, accents, scripts and actual readability of the forms.
- **Distance:** compare reduced unblurred pages/long lines at the same scale.
  Look for dark/light glyphs, uneven line texture and disruptive gaps. These
  are simulated distance views, not a controlled physical reading experiment.
- **Enlarged alphabet:** compare curves, terminals, stress, proportions and
  relations across the whole set. Do not judge only a pair of confusables.
- **Negative space:** inspect the counters and the white between letters as
  shapes, balancing inner and outer space. A white-on-black polarity sample
  alone is not a counterspace study. Equal counter areas are not the goal.
- **Grids:** use baseline, x-height, overshoot and descender guides to locate
  relationships. Optical alignment may intentionally depart from geometric
  equality. A tidy grid is a diagnostic, not a virtue of the font itself.
- **Optical sizes:** compare the actual script and script-script outputs as
  well as enlarged copies. Do not infer absent optical alternates from a frail
  appearance or reward an axis merely because it exists.
- **Direct A/B:** inspect the matched paired detail sheet before concluding
  no change is visible. Cite a specific glyph, proof view and size, not intent.

Use identical content, grid settings, render conditions, scale, crop, polarity
and filtering for both sides. Do not normalize widths, ink density or counters
to conceal differences. Natural proofs must accompany every diagnostic mode.

These tasks operationalize the local research: S01 (Edmondson's proofing),
P02/S02 (fitting and whitespace), P03/P04 (outline craft), P01/P05 (optical
sizes), P07 (script integrity), and M04/M08 (mathematical distinctions).
Specific grids, distance reductions and masks are project proof choices, not
numerical standards prescribed by those authors.

## Apply the eight criteria to this campaign

| Criterion | Greek question |
|---|---|
| R1 | Does the lowercase set have a coherent Greek voice alongside Baskerville, across the declared styles? |
| R2 | Are Greek curves, counters, joins, terminals and stress resolved? |
| R3 | Do Greek fitting strings, meaningful words and formula contexts have balanced inner/outer spacing? |
| R4 | Do the declared Greek text/script/script-script forms survive intended sizes with deliberate optical behaviour? |
| R5 | Are Greek identities and mathematical variant/confusable distinctions clear without distortion? |
| R6 | Do Greek accents, scripts, kerns and attachments compose convincingly? |
| R7 | Do changed Greek forms work inside radicals, fractions, limits, matrices and growing constructions, with unchanged constructions preserved? |
| R8 | Does Greek mathematics sustain an even, authored texture in lines, distance views and Baskerville prose? |

Every below-feature verdict needs constructive feedback: observed shortfall,
proposed change and reason, exact proof/success condition, regression checks
and qualities to preserve. Give one prioritized next experiment. A 3 still
requires release-quality craft with only minor refinements left; repetition
of a serious issue is incompatible with a default 3. Do not force low scores.

The whole Greek campaign uses this same scope and scoring bar for all three
rounds. Whole-font perfection, full language support and actual publication
acceptance are not established by success on these proofs.
